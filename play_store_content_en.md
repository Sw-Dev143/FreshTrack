## 2. Full Description
*(Section that appears in search results and on the app's store page. Enhanced with emojis for attention).*

**FreshTrack: Smart Expiration Date & Medication Tracker**  
Did the expiration date of the products you bought pass? Do you forget when to take which medication? You will no longer waste anything and won't risk your health!

**FreshTrack** is a practical and smart assistant that allows you to easily track all food, deli, cosmetic products, and pharmacy purchases in your home. It warns you before your products expire, thus protecting your budget and preventing waste.

✨ **HIGHLIGHTED FEATURES:**

📷 **Quick Add with Barcode Scanner:** No need to type products one by one! Thanks to the advanced barcode reader, it recognizes thousands of products in seconds.
⏰ **Smart Notification & Warning System:** You receive automatic push notifications 7 days and 3 days before the expiration date, and when it expires.
💊 **Comprehensive Medication Reminder:** Not just kitchen products! Add your medications, daily doses, and times to the system and get warnings when the time comes.
🏷️ **Custom Categories & Colors:** Label your products (Deli, Pharmacy, Care, etc.) to categorize them and filter them visually on the calendar.
🛒 **Smart Shopping List:** Easily add finished or nearly finished products to the shopping list. Mark items easily on the list, and share your list with others if you wish.
💾 **Local Data Backup:** Your data is safe even without internet! Take a JSON backup of all your lists at any time and transfer them to a different device.
🎨 **Dark Mode:** Customize your interface at any time thanks to the stylish and modern night mode that doesn't strain the eyes.

**Contribute to the household economy, prevent food waste!**  
Download FreshTrack now and secure the life of the products in your home.
